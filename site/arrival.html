The Confessions

One platform had a training clause at the end of 2022. By late 2026, more than half of the 25 I tracked did.

For each platform I found the first saved version of its privacy policy or terms of service that contains a training clause: a sentence saying people's information can be used to train, develop or improve AI or machine-learning models. (How I decided what counts is on the Case Notes file.)

The lineup

Click a suspect to turn their card over and read the confession.

The dates cluster. Platform I, a video service, was first, in December 2022. Then came a run in mid-2023, as generative AI went mainstream: four platforms within two months. A second group followed in mid-2024, including three within five weeks of each other. After that the pace slowed, but it didn't stop.
