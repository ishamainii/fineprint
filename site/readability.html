Every document, every revision

Each line is one platform's privacy policy, with a point at every revision. Most drift slowly upward. A few jump by thousands of words in a single revision, usually when a regional section or a new product's terms get folded in.

Reading ease

The Flesch reading-ease score runs from about 0 (very hard) to 100 (very easy), and plain English sits around 60. I took the median sentence score for each document and then the median across documents in force. It went from about 41 to about 38. Three points is small on a 100-point scale, but the decline is steady rather than noise (Kendall τ = −0.76 across 17 quarters), and the whole range sits where reading-ease tables say "college level".

At an average adult reading speed of about 238 words a minute, reading every current privacy policy and terms of service in this set once would take about hours.
