The Stakeout

These documents change all the time. Most edits are tiny; a few rewrite hundreds of sentences at once.

In plain terms

The policy you agreed to has probably changed since you agreed to it. Most edits are tiny, but some rewrite large parts of the document.

A policy you accepted last year is probably not the one in force today. I compared every saved version with the one before it and counted the sentences added or removed. The archive's own maintenance saves and versions with no change are left out.

How big a revision is

Revision sizes are very uneven. Half change sentences or fewer, a typo or a date. But one in ten changes or more, and the largest single revision touched sentences, which amounts to a new document under the old name.
