In plain terms

These policies talk about AI far more than they used to. The older privacy language did not go away. The AI language was added on top of it.

To see how the language shifted, I took the documents in force at the end of each quarter and counted terms per 10,000 words. Measuring the documents actually in force, rather than every saved copy, keeps platforms that revise constantly from dominating the counts.

Twenty-four terms, four years

The AI terms climb steeply from a low base. The older privacy vocabulary (consent, location, advertising, third parties) barely moves. The new language was added on top of the old, not swapped in for it.
